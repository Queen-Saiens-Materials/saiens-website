A material system, not a surface treatment.

OUGER combines high-performance polyvinyl chloride, woven structure, and polymer technology in a four-part assembly designed for texture, stability, and acoustic comfort.

  1. Woven Vinyl Surface

    A richly textured decorative layer designed for water and fire resistance and treated to manage thermal expansion and contraction.

  2. 300°C Heat-Fused Vinyl Composite

    An environmentally considered, low-odor formulation made with imported raw materials for strong bonding and resistance to breakage.

  3. Fiberglass Stabilizing Layer

    Reinforces dimensional stability and helps control the effects of temperature-related movement.

  4. Heat-Fused Vinyl Composite + Felt Backing

    A resilient bonding layer paired with felt to complete the stable, sound-absorbing tile construction.

Architect's FAQ

How is OUGER flooring cleaned?

OUGER flooring is designed for straightforward care. A vacuum, brush, and stain remover can address approximately 99% of routine cleaning needs.

Can a small damaged area be repaired?

Yes. A localized damaged section can be repaired or replaced without removing the full installation.

Can it be installed in bathrooms, on stairs, walls, or ceilings?

OUGER is suitable for dry bathroom areas, but not inside showers. It can be installed on stairs. Wall and ceiling applications should be confirmed only after OUGER has evaluated the installation conditions and the assembly satisfies the fire requirements of the specific project.

Does OUGER flooring contain hazardous substances?

According to OUGER, the flooring does not contain harmful plasticizers or stabilizers such as phthalates and heavy metals, and emissions remain very low throughout the product's service life.

Performance descriptions on this page reflect information published by OUGER. Project teams should request current test reports, installation requirements, and jurisdiction-specific compliance documentation before specification.
